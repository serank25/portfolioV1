The brief

Why Space is a warehouse space-sharing platform that connects businesses needing storage space with warehouse owners and logistics managers who have unused commercial capacity.

The platform is designed to make finding and renting warehouse space faster, simpler, and more flexible. Warehouse owners can list available space with details such as location, capacity, pricing, availability, and facilities, while businesses can search, compare, and request spaces based on their storage requirements.

"The right warehouse should be easier to find, compare, and request — without the uncertainty that usually comes with unused commercial space."

User needs & information architecture

Two sides of the marketplace

The project considers the needs of both businesses looking for storage and warehouse owners or logistics managers with unused commercial capacity. The experience is structured around making the next decision clear for each audience.

  • Warehouse owners can list spaces with location, capacity, pricing, availability, and facilities.
  • Businesses can search and compare spaces against their storage requirements.
  • Both sides have a clear path toward requesting or responding to a space.

Design foundation

The design process combines user research, persona development, information architecture, user journey mapping, wireframing, interaction design, usability testing, and high-fidelity prototyping in Figma.

Information architecture and warehouse listing concept for Why Space.

Design strategy

Based on the project goals, I built the experience around three principles:

Clear listings

Present location, capacity, pricing, availability, and facilities in a consistent format that supports quick comparison.

Requirement-led search

Help businesses search and filter spaces around the requirements that matter to their storage operation.

Trust-building requests

Make it straightforward for businesses to compare suitable spaces and request the right fit with confidence.

Key design decisions

Design for both audiences

The platform serves businesses searching for storage and warehouse owners or logistics managers listing unused capacity. The interface keeps both journeys distinct while making the handoff between them easy to understand.

Make comparison useful

Warehouse listings surface the details that influence a storage decision: location, capacity, pricing, availability, and facilities. This gives businesses a consistent basis for comparing spaces.

Turn interest into a clear request

The request flow is designed to help businesses move from a suitable result to a confident next step, while giving warehouse owners the information they need to respond.

Project outcome

Why Space brings together research-driven UX thinking, structured information architecture, user journey mapping, responsive interface design, and high-fidelity prototyping into one focused warehouse space-sharing concept.

  • Users considered: businesses, warehouse owners, and logistics managers
  • Core actions: list, search, compare, and request warehouse spaces
  • Design tools: Figma and Figma Make
  • Design focus: clarity, flexibility, and confidence in the space-rental process

Reflections

This project reinforced the value of starting with user needs and information architecture before moving into visual details. When a marketplace makes its audiences, requirements, and next steps clear, the experience becomes easier to navigate and more useful for everyone involved.
